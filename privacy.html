Privacy Notice

Claude Exporter — last updated July 28, 2026

This notice explains what data Claude Exporter collects and why. We designed the extension to work without any account system, so we collect as little as possible.

1. What the extension stores

The extension itself never sends your conversation content anywhere — exports are generated and downloaded directly on your own device. The only data it stores (in your browser's local/synced extension storage, never on our servers) is:

  • A random, anonymous device identifier (not linked to your name, email, or Claude account) used solely to recognize your Pro subscription across sessions.
  • How many free exports you've used, and whether Pro is active.
  • Your extension preferences (export format, date/time display, etc.).

2. What this checkout page collects

This page (and the extension's "Upgrade" button) sends only the random device identifier above to our subscription-status service, so it can be linked to your Paddle subscription. No conversation content, no personal identifiers.

3. Payment data

All payment details (card number, billing address, etc.) are collected and processed directly by Paddle.com Market Limited, our payment processor and merchant of record. We never see or store your payment details. See Paddle's Privacy Policy for how they handle your data.

4. Data retention

Subscription-status records are kept only for as long as needed to determine whether your subscription is active, and are removed on request.

5. Your rights

You can request deletion of your subscription-status record at any time by emailing us with the request (see below) — we can locate it only if you also share the device identifier, since we don't collect anything else that could identify you.

6. Contact
